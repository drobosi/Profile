# Hi, I'm Abel Chibuzor Obosi, PhD 👋

**Clinical and health psychologist | Lecturer, University of Ibadan, Nigeria**

[![ORCID](https://img.shields.io/badge/ORCID-0000--0001--9697--8983-A6CE39?style=flat&logo=orcid&logoColor=white)](https://orcid.org/0000-0001-9697-8983)

I teach and supervise research in clinical, health and mental health psychology. My work looks at quality of life, HIV and adolescent wellbeing, trauma and PTSD, substance use, and occupational mental health in Nigerian and wider African settings.

## 🎓 What I do

- **Research supervision:** 30+ BSc projects, 35+ MSc dissertations, and 5 doctoral candidates currently under my supervision
- **Examination:** member of PhD viva voce panels and external examiner for the University of Pretoria, South Africa
- **Clinical practice:** therapist and student counsellor, Behavioural Health Centre, University of Ibadan
- **Research fellow:** African Leishmaniasis Consortium (Early Career Research Fellow, 2025 to present)
- **Editor:** Associate Editor, *Nigerian Journal of Clinical Psychology*

## 🔬 Research interests

- Health-related quality of life
- HIV and adolescent mental health
- Trauma, PTSD and displacement
- Substance use and risky behaviour
- Acceptance and commitment therapy and CBT
- Implementation science
- Climate change and mental health
